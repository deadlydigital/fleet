This page shows the series, not today.

A single brief is a file — briefs/YYYY-MM-DD.md, greppable and diffable — and does not need a web page. What a file cannot show is the same metric across days, which is the only reason to keep briefs at all.

Every figure below is a claim row a pass wrote at the time, with the source and recency it recorded. Nothing here is recomputed. A trend derived from today's database would restate history the moment a source was backfilled.

The brief decides nothing and proposes nothing. There is no LOOKS_WRONG section yet and that is deliberate: judging needs something to judge against, and thresholds derived from history would bless whatever today's numbers happen to be.
