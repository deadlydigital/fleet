Proposals

{# The spec asks for cycles that produced nothing, and their reasons. They are not in the database. Saying so is the only honest thing this page can do. #}
Cycles that produced nothing are not shown, because they are not recorded.

002_proposals.sql creates proposals, proposal_evidence and decisions, and nothing else. cycle_id exists only as a column on proposals, so a cycle that proposed nothing leaves no row anywhere. The “not computed” reasons — the most informative thing the proposer emits at this stage — are assembled in proposer/cycle.py and printed to stdout, reaching journald and nowhere else.

So this page cannot distinguish “the cycle ran and had nothing to say” from “the cycle did not run”. That needs a cycles table, which is a schema change and is logged as a follow-up rather than built here. Until then: journalctl -u fleet-proposer-cycle.service.
